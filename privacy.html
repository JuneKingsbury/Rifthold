Privacy Policy

Rifthold, operated by June Kingsbury (berry branch games). Effective October 6, 2026.

Rifthold is a single-player colony-management game. This policy explains what happens to your data when you choose to use Rifthold's optional Google Drive cloud-save feature. If you never connect a Google account, none of the data handling described below applies to you, and Rifthold keeps your saves only on your own device.

What Google data we access

When you connect Google Drive, Rifthold requests a single, highly restricted permission scope: drive.appdata. This scope grants access only to a hidden, application-specific folder that Rifthold creates inside your own Google Drive. Rifthold cannot see, read, or modify any of your other Google Drive files, folders, documents, or photos. We do not request access to your email, contacts, profile details, or any other Google data.

What we store, and where

  • Save-game data only. The hidden app folder holds nothing but your Rifthold save files (your colony and game state). No personal information is collected or stored there.
  • Sign-in credentials stay on your device. The access tokens that keep you signed in are stored locally on the device running Rifthold. They are never transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by us. Authentication happens directly between your device and Google.
  • We operate no server. Rifthold has no backend that receives your data. Your saves travel directly between your device and your own Google Drive.

How we use the data

The save-game data is used for one purpose only: to let you store your Rifthold saves in your Google Drive and load them back onto your devices. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Rifthold's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting your data

You are in full control and can disconnect at any time:

  • Disconnect Google Drive from within Rifthold, which signs you out and removes the stored tokens from your device.
  • Revoke Rifthold's access entirely from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Rifthold app-data folder (and its save files) from your Google Drive to remove the stored saves permanently.

Children's privacy

Rifthold is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
